Tonight's picks

Two per game
Up to two player props per game, priced at Fanatics (FanDuel's price shown underneath; FanDuel's line when Fanatics doesn't post the prop), −150 to +150, and only ones the model gives at least a 60% chance to win with an edge of 5% or more. Win chance is a log-odds blend of the 5,000 sims and the book's no-vig price. Over the first four graded nights, props below 60% hit under 50% on every night, while those at 60% or more hit 68% to 100%, so a game with nothing that good gets one pick or none instead of a filler. "Top" marks 64% or more. Units: 2 at 66%+, 1.5 at 64–66%, 0.5 at 60–64%. Over the first 105 graded picks, 64%+ went 21-6 while 60–64% went 24-30, so the stake follows the win chance. Players' minutes and per-minute production blend last season with this season's games so far, so new teams, promotions and rookies' real roles count. Moneylines, spreads and totals are priced at Fanatics and compete under the same bar (one per game at most), but over the first 33 graded games the sim's side lost money in all three, so they rarely qualify. Never picked: three-point props (one hot or cold shooting night moves them all together), assist overs, milestone ladders, rookies without NBA stats, questionable players, players the sim can't place in the rotation, and any bet that would have lost in most of his games this season (3+ games played). These rules were chosen on the same four nights they were tested on (29-13 at 60%+, 17-3 at 64%+), so expect real results to come in lower.

Props only count sims where the player plays. A model edge is an opinion, not a guarantee. Bet only what you can afford to lose.

How it works

Player rates

From Basketball-Reference's 2025-26 tables: share of shots at the rim (0–3 ft), short range (3–10 ft), mid-range and three, with make rates in each zone, plus usage, how often each make is assisted, free-throw rate, turnovers, rebounding, steals, blocks and defensive box plus-minus. Small samples shrink toward league averages. Players who missed 2025-26 use 2024-25. Rookies use position priors.

Tonight's context

ESPN rosters and injury designations, plus RotoWire's expected starters and play probabilities. Out players are removed and their minutes and touches flow to whoever is left. Questionable players play in the share of sims their probability says.

Each possession

Lineups rotate from projected minutes. The ball goes to a player by usage, and his matched defender changes both how often he shoots and how well. He turns it over, draws a foul, or shoots from a zone drawn from his own shot profile, shaded by how the defense allowed shots last season. Team defense (points allowed per possession, turnovers forced, free throws and threes allowed) blends last season with this season's games so far; this season counts half once a team has played 10 games. Makes are assisted at his real assisted rate, and the passer is chosen by assist rate. Misses go to the offensive or defensive glass by rebound rates.

Fouls, blowouts and variance

Every foul is charged to a player at his own foul rate. Coaches sit players at 2 fouls in Q1, 3 in the first half, 4 in Q3 and 5 in Q4, and a sixth foul ends his night. Starters come out mid-game in blowouts (a lead of 24 or more in Q4, 18 with 8 minutes left, 13 with 3 left). Players and teams can run hot or cold on a given night, so prop ranges are as wide as real games. About 28% of simulated games end with a 20+ point margin, in line with the first three nights (8 of 25), and starters average about 27 minutes in those sims, as they did in the real ones. Starter minutes are projected from last season's minutes with no extra bump, since those already include blowouts.

Coaching and clock

Starters play more in close fourth quarters. Trailing teams foul late and hunt threes, and ties go to overtime. Pace blends both teams' 2025-26 pace. One league-wide scoring constant keeps totals on a 2025-26 scoring level. It is never fit to tonight's lines.

RotoBot hasn't published its model code. This sim follows the method it describes: real 2025-26 shot locations, make rates, passing and matchups, played out possession by possession, 5,000 times per game. "Most typical game" replays the sim closest to the median result, like RotoBot's video.
